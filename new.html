Bloom Study Garden
Version 1.0 · Built for focused learners

Bloom turns your study sessions into a living garden. Every time you complete a focus session, a new flower blooms — a gentle reminder that small, consistent effort grows into something beautiful.

Features
🍅 Focus Timer
Pomodoro-style sessions with customizable durations
🌻 Garden
Visual progress tracker — one flower per session
✅ Tasks
Priority-based to-do list to stay organized
📅 Schedule
Event calendar to plan your study days
💡 Tip: Complete at least 4 focus sessions a day to grow a full row in your garden. Your data is saved locally in your browser.
